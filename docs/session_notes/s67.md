# Session 67: Sky, light and colour (polish pass)

Built from docs/polish_plan.md items L1 to L8 (PRD "Polish pass", Light and colour). Everything
new has a switch in game_config.gd, and the old look comes back with the switches off.

## What works

- **L1 Sharp ground far ahead** (sun_cycle.gd `_apply_polish_graphics`): texture filtering at
  low angles 16x on Medium and High, 4x on Low (`ANISO_LEVEL`). Debanding (no stripes in smooth
  gradients) on the Mac on Medium and High (`DEBANDING_ON`; the browser can't do it). The sky
  shader also adds a tiny bit of noise to its gradient, which stops banding on every renderer.
- **L2 Our own cartoon sky** (`assets/shaders/sky_stylized.gdshader` + `scripts/sky_look.gd`,
  switch `SKY_STYLIZED`). The same gradient as before (same colours from LIGHT_KEYS, same curve),
  plus a round glowing sun disc and soft halo, and flat-bottomed cartoon clouds with shaded
  bottoms that drift with the game clock. Clouds are peach in the morning, white at noon and
  gold with pink bottoms at sunset (from the sun colour and height). Clouds fade out near the
  horizon. No clouds on Low. The lighting pass (`AT_CUBEMAP_PASS`) skips clouds and the disc,
  so the light in the shadows stays as warm as before. The old ProceduralSkyMaterial is kept
  on the Sky (meta `old_sky`) and comes back with the switch off.
- **L3 Colour grade** (`tools/make_luts.py` → `assets/luts/neutral.png`, `golden.png`,
  `fresh.png`, 33x33x33 cubes as 1089x33 strips, imported as Texture3D). Set in sun_cycle
  `_apply_look()` from `LOOK_LUT` (default **"golden"**, the plan's pick; "neutral" = off).
  With a LUT on, saturation is 1.25 instead of 1.3 (`LOOK_LUT_SATURATION`), because the LUT adds
  a little colour. Golden: sunny parts warmer (red x1.04, blue x0.95 in the highlights), shade a
  little teal, a gentle S-curve, a bit more colour for dull colours. Fresh: the same idea, much
  less warm, a bit more pop.
- **L4 Vignette** (`assets/shaders/screen_grade.gdshader`, added by look_extras as CanvasLayer
  "Vignette", layer -1: over the 3D world, under the HUD). `VIGNETTE_STRENGTH` 0.22, 0 = off.
- **L5 Warm bounce light (SSIL)** on High on the Mac only (`SSIL_*`), half size, medium quality.
  `WEB_SSAO` (false) lets the browser try the simple corner shadows on Medium later.
- **L6 Light shafts** (`scripts/light_shafts.gd` + `assets/shaders/light_shaft.gdshader`):
  soft additive beams with drifting dust through the shop glass of the Looks-pass rooms
  (restaurants and the two shops, 12 rooms). A pool of 24 beams (2 per room) goes to the rooms
  nearest the camera, only drawn within 40 m, Medium and High only. Each beam edge stops at the
  floor or a wall, so no beam pokes out of its room. Beams go mostly into the room (leaning up to
  0.6 sideways with the sun, sloping down 0.35 to 0.75), so they reach the tables instead of hugging
  the glass. Strength 0.7 (`SHAFT_ALPHA`; the plan's 0.18 was invisible in the bright rooms,
  because the beams add light to near-white walls). Strong at 8 to 9:30 and 16 to 18, faint
  (30%) around noon (`SHAFT_HOUR_KEYS`). They follow the sun every quarter game hour.
- **L7 Cloud shadows** (`scripts/cloud_shadows.gd`): built but **off** (`CLOUD_SHADOWS := false`),
  as the plan says. One big downward decal with a soft noise picture, drifting the same way as
  the clouds. Not measured on a real GPU yet.
- **L8 Outlines** (`scripts/outline_look.gd` + `assets/shaders/outline_post.gdshader`): our own
  depth-only edge lines (dark teal #1B3A40 at 60%, about 1.5 px at 720p and as thick on bigger
  screens, fading from 40 to 80 m; the plan's 40% and 1 px were too faint to see), so it works in the
  browser too. **Off by default**: only drawn when `Settings.outlines` is on (Session 71 adds the
  button if Louis wants it).

## What I chose, and why

- **LUT sampling**: I checked in Godot how it reads the cube (a probe render with an identity LUT):
  Godot reads square i for colours around (i + 0.5) / 33 (no half-square correction). The LUTs are
  made for exactly that, with the end squares holding pure black and white, so "neutral" changes
  the picture by at most 1/255 (measured) and black stays black.
- **Cloud drift from the game clock**, not the shader's TIME: a sky shader that uses TIME makes
  Godot redraw the lighting picture of the sky every frame. The cloud position updates with the
  sky colours (every 1.5 s), which Godot already does today, so the clouds cost nothing extra in
  the lighting. They move very slowly, so the small steps aren't visible.
- **Shafts go to the nearest rooms** instead of the first 24 rooms found, so every shop you walk
  past has its beams. Shafts are faked into the room even when the sun is behind the shop (a
  cosy look beats strict physics here). **Plaza passages have no shafts**: the 24-beam budget goes
  to the rooms, and the passages are open (no room box to keep beams inside).
- **Clouds**: about a third of the sky (cover 0.52, scale 2.0), crisp cartoon edges.
