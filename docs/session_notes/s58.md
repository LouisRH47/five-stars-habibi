# Session 58: The first 6 insides and the food changes

## What works
- **A real room template** (`scripts/interior_room.gd`, Appendix B): every Plaza restaurant is a
  12 x 10 m room with a 4.5 m ceiling (Grand Groceries 24 x 10 m). Dining from the glass to 7 m in
  (about 75% of the room), the counter at 7.2 m in, the kitchen behind it, the menu board on the
  back wall with the logo and real items with Dh prices (`data/interior_menus.gd`). Tiled floor in
  the shop's colours, a coloured band round the walls, a drinks fridge by the counter end, a bin by
  the door, plants, 3 or 4 warm hanging lamps (one casts soft shadows on Medium/High).
- **Lots of seats with people eating** (`scripts/interior_dining.gd`): window bar with stools along
  the glass, a wall bench with 2-seat tables, 4-seat tables; 23 to 30 seats per restaurant, about
  half with someone sitting and a plate of the shop's food (or a coffee) in front of them.
- **Three people at work in every room**: the one at the pickup end who hands you the bag
  (`CounterStaff`, as before), the cashier at the till, and the cook. The pickup spot is separate
  from the till and has a hanging "Pick up · استلام الطلبات" sign.
- **The six rooms (Appendix B), each based on how the real kind of place is laid out:**
  - **1000 Pizza**: a round dome oven (1.6 m across, 1.8 m tall) covered in red and gold mosaic
    tiles, on a round brick base with logs stacked in front, an arched mouth with fire, flames,
    flickering glow and embers, a chimney pipe to the ceiling, turned so the fire looks at the door.
    The pizza maker slides a pizza in with a long peel every 10 s. Marble prep counter with dough
    balls and topping tubs, a stack of pizza boxes, brick back wall, black-and-white floor, red
    chairs, a framed photo, basil pots.
  - **Brain Freeze Gelato**: the curved glass gelato case (3 m long, 1.2 m tall) 2 m in from the
    door and facing it, 12 tubs in 2 rows, each with its flavour tag (Pistachio, Mango, Chocolate,
    Strawberry, Vanilla, Lemon, Cookies, Caramel, Coconut, Mint, Blueberry, Date). The server
    behind it scoops (the scoop dips in and out); the till is at the end of the case; cones,
    cups and topping jars on the back counter; the menu hangs over it facing the door. Pink and
    white striped walls, pastel floor, a big cone sign, the gelato lab at the back. 30 seats.
  - **Spill the Brew**: a real coffee shop. A two-group espresso machine **on the counter top**
    (chrome with red sides, cups stacked on top, steam puffs from the wand), two grinders beside
    it, the milk fridge under the counter, a glass pastry case by the till (croissants, cookies and
    3 cakes on stands), bags of beans on shelves, the barista facing the customers. Window stools,
    a sofa corner on a rug with armchairs and a coffee table, round cafe tables, a plant wall.
  - **Shawarma Supreme**: smaller and fuller. Two spinning spits (chicken and meat) in the front
    window behind a long counter down the right side (with Arabic patterned tiles), the cook
    slicing, bread stack, sauce bottles, wraps ready to go, a fries station, a blender for fresh
    juice, two juice fridges, and a **lit picture menu across the whole back wall** (5 dishes with
    pictures and prices). 27 seats.
  - **Bread & Zaatar**: a manakish bakery, no cakes. A stone oven with a wide low mouth and fire
    glow on a stone plinth, a copper hood, the baker sliding flatbreads in on a long peel, the dough
    table with rolled-out rounds and bowls of zaatar, cheese and chilli, a wooden rack of khubz,
    baskets of bread on the counter, hanging bread baskets, a long shared table. 28 seats.
  - **Grand Groceries** (`scripts/interior_market.gd`): a real supermarket. Fruit and veg straight
    inside the entrance, 10 drinks fridges along the back wall, the bread shelf on the left wall,
    3 shelf units with yellow price strips and hanging bilingual aisle signs (1 Snacks, 2 Rice and
    pasta, 3 Home), 2 checkouts by the exit door with belts, tills and sweets racks, drinks coolers,
    offer boards (Fruit box 25 Dh, Water 6 pack 9 Dh), bright white panel lights, shoppers pushing
    trolleys round the aisles, a shelf stacker. You pick up at checkout 1.
- **Machines from shapes** (`scripts/interior_machines.gd`, sizes from Appendix H), because no CC0
  pack has them: pizza dome oven, gelato case, espresso machine, grinder, pastry case, shawarma
  spit, manakish oven. They stand on the floor or on a counter top; the test checks it.
- **Free kit models** (KayKit, Kenney, via `KitProps`): chairs, bar stools, tables (resized to
  real table sizes), sofa, armchairs, rug, kitchen counters and fridges, plants, bins, picture
  frame, jars, food on plates (pizza, croissants, cakes, fries, sandwiches, bread...), cups,
  supermarket shelves, fridges, fruit and bread stands, trolleys. Repeated chairs and food are drawn
  together (one MultiMesh per model per room).
- **Moving parts** (`scripts/venue_motion.gd`): fire flicker and embers, the peels, the gelato scoop,
  espresso steam, the two spits turning with the knife slicing, shoppers walking with trolleys.
  Only rooms within 34 m of the camera are busy: further away the people, lamps, moving parts and
  sound are switched off (Appendix I).
- **Room sounds** (`tools/make_sounds.py`, new files `assets/audio/room_*.wav`, all made in code):
  oven crackle, freezer hum, espresso grind and steam, grill sizzle, bakery chatter, supermarket
  till beeps (plus wok, sushi belt, workshop and shop music for Session 59). Quiet, looped, played
  from inside the room so they fade in as you walk through the door. The older sounds come out
  byte-for-byte the same (the new ones use their own random numbers).
- **A short queue** (`scripts/counter_queue.gd`): about 1 time in 3, when you walk in for a pickup,
  1 or 2 people are at the till in front of you. You wait 2 to 4 s (the side panel says "Wait a
  moment: 1 in front of you"), then they walk out and holding E works.
- **Food changes (Appendix F)**: Spill the Brew sells iced latte, flat white, croissant and cake;
  Bread & Zaatar sells manakish, khubz bread and fatayer (no cake). The fragile cake comes from
  Spill the Brew; Auntie Mariam orders an iced latte; Ms Dana's cake comes from Spill the Brew.
  Meter numbers: iced latte = bubble tea's numbers (spills most); flat white goes cold and spills a
  little; croissant easy; bread items go cold slowly and are easy. Mariam's "extra pearls" lines
  are now about her iced latte. **Old saves**: "bubble tea" in the history is read as "iced latte";
  regular visit counts are kept.

## Choices I made (and why)
- **Kept the old rooms**: `GameConfig.INTERIORS_LOOKS_PASS = false` brings back the old plain rooms
  (VenueBuilder's old code is untouched); `GameConfig.FOOD_CHANGES_ON = false` and
  `FOOD_CHANGES = False` in tools/build_wahat_data.py bring back the old foods. Bubble tea stays in
  the meter tables (the test area's Boba Oasis still sells it).
- **Where things are**: the counter front is at z = -2.2 in room directions (7.2 m in). Pickup spots
  are in `InteriorMenus.PICKUP`; `VenueBuilder.counter_of()` returns them for Plaza restaurants and
  `PlaceBuilder.restaurant_counter()` now uses `counter_of()` (one line).
- **Pizza oven turned 29 degrees** so the fire is seen from the door over the counter (at 90
  degrees, facing the kitchen, you only saw the top of the dome).
- **Manakish oven 1 m deep, not 1.4 m, on a 0.35 m plinth**: the kitchen is only 2 m deep, so the
  baker needs room in front, and the plinth lifts the mouth so you see the fire over the counter.
- **Shawarma counter down the right side** (not across the back): the plan wants the spits in the
  front window by the counter and a picture menu across the whole back wall; a side counter does
  both, like many real shawarma shops.
- **Gelato**: the server zone is closed in (case in front, till counter right, a short counter left,
  back counter behind); the menu hangs over the back counter facing the door.
- **Seats per room**: the plan says 20 to 28; Brain Freeze has 30 (round tables at the sides plus
  4-seat tables at the back) because its front is taken by the case.
- **Queue**: it only appears once per order, when you are inside the room; `CounterQueue.enabled`
  is switched off in the smoke test because it checks exact 1.5 s E holds. It blocks the E hold
  only (DeliveryManager._update_hold, 3 lines), not `try_interact()` (tests and the bot call it).
- **Staff wear t-shirts with a cap** in the shop's colour (the kandura/abaya looks don't go under
  a cap).
- **Web build**: the Web preset no longer leaves out all of `assets/kits/`. `tools/web_kit_filter.py`
  finds every model name used in scripts/ and data/ and writes the exclude filter: unused packs out
  whole, unused models in used packs out by name. Re-run it after using a new model or merging.
  **Measured Web pack: 13.6 MB (13.0 MiB)** with the 42 models Session 58 uses (limit 25 MiB):
  `godot --headless --export-pack "Web" /tmp/web.pck` (no export templates needed for a pack).

## Known issues
- Diners sit still (no eating arm movement yet); they have plates and cups in front of them.
- The supermarket shelves are Kenney Mini Market models stretched to 1 m deep gondolas.
- Frame rate not measured on a Mac. Each restaurant has about 15 seated people; rooms further than
  34 m hide them, but you can see 3 or 4 busy rooms at once in the Plaza. If Medium is slow there,
  lower `INTERIOR_DINER_SHARE` or `INTERIOR_ACTIVE_DISTANCE`.
