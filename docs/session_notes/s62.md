# Session 62 — Car parks and kerbs (4 November 2026)

Built by Claude on the `looks-s62` branch.

## What works
- **Louis's rows.** Every car park is now full of bays: 1 row, a driving aisle, 2 rows back to
  back, another aisle, 1 row (Appendix C, "full": 32 m). Bays 2.5 x 5 m with white lines,
  aisles 6 m with two-way arrows and a white give-way line at the ring road.
- **Solar roofs over every row**: one roof over each single row (5.5 m deep, 2.6 m at the aisle
  side, 3.0 m at the back, posts on the back kerb) and one shared roof over each back-to-back pair
  (10.5 m, highest down the middle, posts down the middle line like a real double carport).
  Dark blue panels with a thin grid. 19 roofs in 7 car parks. The posts are solid.
- **About half the bays have a car** (exactly 50%: 416 cars in 832 bays): random Kenney car
  (sedan, SUV, hatchback, luxury SUV, van) and a random colour (white, black, silver, red, blue,
  beige, green, orange…). The shader keeps windows, lights and wheels and only repaints the body.
- **Tight against the clusters.** Between a cluster's outside lane and the first row there's only
  a kerb, a little **buggy lane** (light paving) and a kerb: 3.5 m. The buggy lane runs the whole
  length, joins the cluster's cross lane near the ring road (a short "Buggy Link") and the spine
  road at the other end. It's in ROADS ("C1 Car Park Buggy Lane" etc.), so Session 64's buggies
  can use it. Cars never use it.
- **Kerbstones** (0.3 m wide, 0.15 m high, light grey): round every car park, both sides of every
  buggy lane, at the row ends, and **along both edges of the ring road all the way round**
  (the job Session 53 left for this session), with an opening at every lane, path, car park
  aisle and horse crossing (4.7 km of kerb). Only for looks: no collision, the bike rides over.
- **Entrances**: a grass verge with a kerb between the ring road and the first bays; each aisle
  opens straight onto the ring road. Two electric car chargers per car park and a bilingual
  sign ("P · Cluster 1 Parking · مواقف المجموعة ١").
- **Freed space** (the old car parks were wider and longer) is grass beds with real Session 55
  grass and 135 date palms. The C4–C5 building site moved to the middle, between the two new
  car parks there.
- **Visiting cars**: one per car park. It waits in its own free bay by the first aisle, drives
  out through the aisle, goes once round the ring road and parks again.
- Asphalt uses the Session 55 stylized asphalt texture (TerrainMaterials "road").

## Choices I made (and why)
- **Narrow version.** The gaps C1–C2 and C2–C3 are 29 m between the clusters' outside lanes.
  The plan's narrow version ("2 back to back + aisle + 1 row") can't work: one of the back-to-back
  rows would have no aisle, so no car could get in. I used **1 row, aisle, 2 rows back to back,
  aisle** (27 m) with 5.8 m aisles, a 1.5 m buggy lane on one side and kerbs: exactly 29 m. It's
  Louis's 1-2-1 minus the last row. If Louis wants the full 1-2-1 there, the clusters need to
  move about 5 m apart.
- **"Within 3.5 m"** is measured from the cluster's outside lane (the lane at the edge of the
  cluster), not from the home walls: the homes on the edge of a cluster face that 6 m lane and
  have a 4 m front strip, so the nearest bay is about 13.5 m from their walls. Moving the lanes
  would break the clusters, so I didn't.
- **Cluster 3 is the one place the 85 m rule fails.** Its homes run right up to the east ring
  road, so the only car park is to the west (C2–C3). 29 homes on C3's east side are 86–120 m
  from it. The only free space near them is the north-east corner of the spine lawn (about
  40 x 32 m, x 376–415, z -37 to -3: a full 1-2-1 would fit), but that's the spine (Session 63's
  area) so I left it. **Louis: do you want a small car park there?** The car park test reports
  this as "KNOWN" and passes; every other home is within 85 m.
- **Kerbs have no collision** (like the speed bumps, a 15 cm step would catch the bike's box).
- **Parked cars are drawn in batches** (CarParkCars, a MultiMesh per car park and model): 416
  separate car models would be about 2,000 draw calls. Each car still has its own solid body
  (ParkedCar_<n>), so crashes work as before. Cars further than 260 m aren't drawn.
- One ring road lamp post stood in a C5 West aisle: lamp posts now step along the road out of
  any aisle. The old car park ticket machine and bike rack (district_detail) now stand on the
  verge instead of in a bay or aisle.

## Nothing deleted
The old data is kept as PARKING_AREAS_OLD and PARKED_CARS_OLD; the old per-car roofs, asphalt pad
and bay lines still work with CARPARK_ROWS = false.

## Checks
- New tests/carpark_test.gd (22 checks, in the browser build's suite list): 1-2-1 rows (1-2 in the
  narrow gaps), bay sizes, no bay overlaps another, a roof over every row (data and built, a roof
  really overhead), roofs 0–3 m on posts, no post/charger/sign/lamp/rack in an aisle, every parked
  car inside one bay, one car per bay, the visitor's bay free, 40–60% full, every car park within
  3.5 m of its cluster, 85 m from every home (C3 reported as KNOWN), buggy lanes join roads at both
  ends and never the ring road, no car park or grass bed overlaps a road/home/building, kerbs along
  every car park and both sides of the ring road with openings at every lane end and aisle,
  visiting cars never on a buggy lane, every parked car has a body and is drawn.
- Smoke test PASS (new check: every row has its roof, kerbs built). Layout test PASS.
  district_traffic PASS (the roof count check now counts row roofs), detail_world, grass,
  static_batch, navigation, crash_impact, minimap_cache PASS.
