# Session 64: Buggies everywhere and building sites

## What works

**Buggies (golf buggies) drive all over the community, never on the ring road.**
- New `scripts/buggy_roads.gd` (BuggyRoads): the buggy road map, built from the ROADS list
  (plus an optional `BUGGY_LANES` list if a later session adds one). Every crossing and
  T-junction is a junction; the stretches between are the lanes buggies use.
  - Left out: the black roads (`GameConfig.BLACK_ROADS`: ring road, gate roads, highway, bus
    road) **and a 6 m strip beside them** (`BUGGY_BLACK_ROAD_GAP`), so a buggy is never on or
    along a black road. Lanes that run into the ring road stop 6 m before it; the outer cross
    lane of each cluster (3 m from the ring road kerb) and Stables Road (5 m from it) are left
    out completely. Buggies never cross a black road.
  - Left out by name (`BUGGY_NO_GO_WORDS`): anything with "walk", "track", "domes path",
    "door", "horse", "bridle", "jog" in its name (the red walking track, bike and horse tracks,
    door paths). Paths under 3 m wide are left out, except roads with "Buggy" in the name
    (the car park buggy lanes from Session 62, 1.5 to 2.9 m wide, down to 1.4 m).
  - When the world is built (2 physics frames later) LifeBuilder runs `check_clear()`: it
    sweeps a buggy-sized box along both sides of every stretch and drops any stretch with
    something solid on it. Today that drops 17 stretches: the mini-plaza bits of each
    cluster's Alley (benches, playground, cooling tower), one bit of Plaza to C1 through the
    C Institute, and the bottom of C1 Link (a palm, see known issues). Then only the biggest
    joined-up part is kept.
  - It's all data-driven: after merging, the Session 63 "Spine Buggy Road", the Session 62
    car park buggy lanes and the new "C3 East Car Park Buggy Lane" were picked up
    automatically; the "Spine Walking Track" is left out (its name has "walk"/"track").
  - Today's map: 142 junctions, 35 road names (all cluster lanes, links, alleys, cross row
    alleys, Spine Path, Plaza to C1, South Spine Link, Western Link, School Road, the car park
    buggy lanes...). About 9x the length of the old fixed routes.
- New `scripts/buggy_driver.gd` (BuggyDriver, extends RouteMover): picks a random junction at
  least 25 m away, drives there on the shortest way, keeping **1.5 m right of the lane
  middle** (less on narrow paths, and 0.85 m where it squeezes between the bollards at lane
  entrances), cuts corners a little so turns are smooth, slows down for corners, waits 2 to 6 s,
  then picks the next place. A turn back the way it came is a small U-turn.
- Stopping: like every RouteMover it stops for the bike, the rider on foot, people, kids, cats
  and road works in front (look-ahead 3 m, so it stops about 4 m before you). New: it also
  checks where it will be over the next 3.6 m of its route (round corners and U-turns) against
  every other buggy's footprint, so buggies never drive into each other at junctions. If
  something stays in the way for 5 s (`LIFE_GIVE_UP_TIME`) it turns round and plans a new trip
  that doesn't use that stretch.
- 14 buggies (`BUGGIES` in the generator): 5 on Low (one per cluster), 10 on Medium, 14 on High.
  Speeds 10 to 14 km/h each. Riders: a resident, two residents, or a gardener in green with a
  cap and tools in the back (green bin, soil sack, rake, spade).
- The old fixed routes are kept as `BUGGY_FIXED_ROUTES`; `GameConfig.BUGGIES_ROAM = false` goes
  back to them.

**Building sites get a half-built building.** New `scripts/building_site.gd` (BuildingSite),
called from `DistrictDetail._site()` for every BUILDING_SITES area:
- A concrete frame, 2 floors on narrow sites (15 to 16 m wide) and 3 on the others: columns
  every ~5 m, floor slabs, ground floor walled in with block walls and window gaps, the next
  floor walled on the back and one end, the top slab only half poured with shuttering boards on
  the other half, and steel bars sticking up from the top columns.
- Scaffolding along the front and one end: upright poles every 2 m in two rows, rails every
  2 m up, cross pieces, boards and toe boards on every lift, cross braces, green see-through
  safety net (full height on the end, top band only on the front so the building shows). All
  poles are one MultiMesh per site (134 to 210 poles).
- The old crane (same shapes, plus a base block), jib along the site's long side, a site cabin
  (the old white box with a blue band, door, windows, step), a portable toilet, 3 pallets of
  blocks, a pile of orange pipes, a bundle of steel bars, the old sand piles, a cement mixer,
  and a "Phase 2 coming soon · المرحلة الثانية قريباً" hoarding on the front fence.
- 3 workers in hi-vis shirts and white hard hats: one walks up and down the front, one stands
  on the scaffolding boards, one at the mixer. They stop animating beyond 120 m.
- The fence and the "Coming soon" sign stay as before. The big site (site 0, built by
  LandmarkBuilder with its own hoarding, crane and containers) gets the building in a new
  `work_area` in `BUILDING_SITE` (x -133 to -27, z 140 to 188), clear of its crane, containers
  and sand.
- Solid: the frame and scaffolding have collision boxes (you can't ride in).
- Old version kept: `GameConfig.SITE_HALF_BUILT = false`.
- No CC0 model kit used (none has scaffolding or cranes), so nothing changes in the Web export.

## Choices (and why)
- **Spine walkers:** before Session 63 was merged, the walkers walked in the buggies' lanes, so
  I'd moved them to Domes Path. Session 63 moved them onto the red walking track, and the merge
  keeps Session 63's PEOPLE_ROUTES (and HORSE_RIDERS).
- "Along" a black road: I took it as "within 6 m of its edge". The test fails if any lane
  middle is within 5.5 m of a black road, or a buggy gets within 4 m of one in 600 s of driving.
- Keep-right of 1.5 m (plan) is used on 6 m lanes; it drops to 0.85 m between the 4 m-apart
  bollards at lane entrances (otherwise the buggy would hit them), 0.7 m on 3 m alleys and
  the middle on the narrow car park buggy lanes.
- Buggies use the junction graph, not the navigation grid: the grid is 1 m squares and slow to
  scan; the graph is ~140 junctions and plans in microseconds. navigation_grid.gd wasn't changed.
  The test checks every driving line on the same physics scan the smoke test uses.

## Frame rate
Couldn't measure on Louis's Mac here. What it costs: per buggy one physics shape query per
physics frame (as before) plus a few 2D box checks against buggies within ~7 m; 14 buggies on
High (was 6), 5 on Low. Per site: about 150 plain boxes (merged by StaticBatch), 1 MultiMesh,
2 see-through net panels (2 extra draws), 3 animated workers that stop beyond 120 m. Total
over 5 sites: 10 net draws, 5 pole MultiMeshes, 15 workers.

## Known issues
- A palm (Palm_4 at x 50, z about -8) stands inside C1 Link (x 45 to 51), so buggies can't use the
  last bit of C1 Link down to the spine (they go round via Plaza to C1). The layout check
  doesn't count palms; worth moving the palm.
- South Spine Link runs 1 m into the old gym spot; now that Session 60 moved the gym, that
  stretch is open again.
- Walkers and buggies still meet at crossings (buggy waits). If a buggy meets something on a
  narrow car park buggy lane head on, one turns round after 5 s.
- Tests must not name BuggyDriver/BuggyRoads/BuildingSite as types: with `-s`, that makes Godot
  compile walker.gd before the GameState autoload exists and the walker breaks. The tests use
  untyped access.

## Louis's check
1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**.
2. Ride into any cluster (for example Cluster 2, north of the spine). Within a minute you'll
   see golf buggies driving along the lanes, on the right-hand side, some with two people,
   some with a gardener and tools in the back. They slow down at corners.
3. Stop your bike in front of one: it stops about 4 m before you and waits. Stay there and
   after 5 seconds it turns round and goes another way.
4. Ride to the ring road (the big black road round the edge): you won't see a buggy on it or
   driving along it.
5. Ride to a building site (for example the one between the Cluster 4 and Cluster 5 car parks,
   or the big one west of Cluster 4): a half-built grey concrete building with scaffolding,
   boards and green net, a yellow crane, a cabin, piles of blocks and pipes, a cement mixer, a
   "Phase 2 coming soon" board and workers in orange and yellow shirts with white hard hats.

## Fix (after Session 58 was merged): "Buggy_6 gets stuck"
Nothing new was in the way and Session 58 didn't touch the buggies. Buggy_6 met Buggy_9 head on
in the narrow C1 Car Park Buggy Lane, which has a dead end at its north end. Both turned round
after 5 s, so Buggy_6 drove 90 m back into the dead end, found no way out, and drove straight
back down the same lane. That round trip took almost exactly a minute, so at the test's minute
mark it was 1 m from where it had been a minute before and the test called it stuck. (The map
change that made this happen was most likely the Session 65 palm move, which opened C1 Link
again and changed the buggies' trips; the cats and walkers start at random spots, so it failed
on some runs and not others.) Fixes: (1) game: `BuggyRoads.dead_end_behind()` spots when the way
back only leads into a dead end; then a buggy blocked by another buggy keeps waiting instead of
turning round, the one going in turns round, and the first one follows it out
(`BUGGY_DEAD_END_GIVE_WAY`, waits at most `BUGGY_DEAD_END_WAIT` = 15 s, then turns round as
before). (2) test: "stuck" now means it never got 3 m from its spot during the minute (a buggy
that turned round for a cat once came back past the same spot at the minute mark too), and a
new check drives two buggies head on into the C2 Car Park Buggy Lane. buggy_route PASS 3 runs in
a row (and the failing random seeds pass on a 20-minute drive), smoke, navigation,
district_traffic and layout PASS.
