# Session 56: The free asset packs

## What works
- 15 CC0 model packs are in `assets/kits/<pack>/` (1,221 models, 62 MB on disk with Godot's
  `.import` files, 57 MB without). Every folder has its licence file. Godot imports all of them
  with no errors (`--import` twice: the first run only shows the usual font bootstrap lines).
- `assets/kits/sources.json`: for every pack the official page, where the licence was checked,
  the exact download (git repository + commit + folder) and the SHA-256 of every file.
  Remake it after adding files: `python3 tools/kit_sources.py`.
- `CREDITS.md` has a new "Free model packs (Session 56)" section crediting every pack.
- `scripts/kit_props.gd` (class `KitProps`), the loader:
  - `KitProps.place(parent, "kaykit_restaurant/stove_multi", pos, facing, scale := 1.0, snap := "")`
    returns a holder node. `facing` is in degrees like the data lists (0 = front looks along +Z;
    every pack's front is already +Z, checked in the renders). `scale` is on top of real size.
  - Snaps: `floor` (bottom on pos.y), `counter` (bottom on pos.y + 1.05 m), `wall` (back
    touches pos, front points along facing), `hang` (top touches pos.y, a ceiling), and
    `origin` (the model's own origin on pos: for crops and trees whose roots go under the
    ground). Left empty, the catalogue's / pack's usual snap is used.
  - Every placed prop is in the group `kit_prop` with metadata `kit_id`, `kit_snap`,
    `kit_snap_y`, so a layout check can test it.
  - Loaded files are cached (300 chairs = the chair file loaded once).
  - `KitProps.place_many(parent, id, spots, facings, scale, snap)`: lots of copies of one
    model drawn as one MultiMesh per part (50 chairs = 1 draw call). This is the "don't cost
    300 draw calls" step of the plan. I did it in kit_props.gd instead of changing
    static_batch.gd, because StaticBatch only merges plain coloured PrimitiveMeshes, and the
    kit models are textured ArrayMeshes (and it keeps static_batch.gd out of the merge).
  - `KitProps.size_of(id)` gives a model's real size in metres (w x h x d).
- `data/kit_catalogue.gd` (class `KitCatalogue`): `PACKS` (folder -> maker, name, metres per
  unit) and `MODELS` (193 picked models -> what it is, real height, size fix, snap).
- `scenes/kit_preview.tscn` (+ `scripts/kit_preview.gd`): every catalogue model at real size,
  one row per pack with its name on the floor; counter things on 1.05 m counters, ceiling
  things on a beam, wall things against a wall; the animals play their idle animation.
- `tests/kit_props_test.gd` (added to tools/build-web.sh): every catalogue model loads, is
  within a third of its real height, and sits right for its snap (within 2 cm); every snap mode
  lands right; place_many makes one MultiMesh per part on the floor; the cache works; the
  animation library has swim, sit, jog, walk and idle clips; every pack has a licence file and
  a CC0 sources.json entry. `-- --list` prints the size in metres of every one of the 1,221
  models (handy when picking new ones).
- Nothing in the game world uses the kits yet (Sessions 58 to 64 will).

## Important: the web build
The Web export preset now excludes `assets/kits/*` and `scenes/kit_preview.tscn`.
Cloudflare Pages refuses any file of 25 MiB or more (tools/prepare_web.py), and the kits would
push index.pck from 11.6 MB (measured with the exclusion) to about 35 MB (measured: a desktop
pack with the kits is 34.8 MB). Desktop presets still include everything.
**When Sessions 58 to 64 start using a model**, the web build needs it too: change the Web
exclude filter to leave out only the unused packs (or list the used files), and keep index.pck
under 25 MiB. A model the web build can't find shows nothing there, with a warning.

## Choices and numbers
- Metres per unit (data/kit_catalogue.gd PACKS): KayKit restaurant and furniture 0.8 (a
  compromise: chairs 0.97 m, tables 0.8 m, counters 0.8 m, fridges 2.0 m, walls 3.2 m);
  KayKit city 4.5; Kenney food 0.35; Kenney furniture 2.1; Kenney nature 2.5; Kenney city
  roads and commercial 8.0; Kenney mini market 2.0; Quaternius nature 1.5; crops 0.6;
  animals 1.0 (each animal has its own fix, e.g. horse 0.4); people 1.0 (already metres).
  Things that came out wrong get a per-model `scale` in MODELS (KayKit food, pots and crates
  are giant, Kenney flowers and grass too tall, Kenney palms too short...).
- Counter height for the "counter" snap: `GameConfig.KIT_COUNTER_HEIGHT = 1.05` (new section
  "Session 56: the free asset packs" at the end of game_config.gd).
- Added an "origin" snap (not in the plan): the crops have roots below ground level, so
  sitting their lowest point on the floor would lift them out of the bed.
- KayKit kitchen pieces are chunky: a counter is 1.6 m long and 1.6 m deep at our scale.
  Sessions 58/59 may want to squash them in depth (scale the holder's Model node on Z).
- No retargeting yet (the task said optional, and person.gd isn't touched). The UAL
  skeleton uses Rigify names (`root`, `DEF-hips`, `DEF-spine.001`... 53 bones), so a BoneMap to
  Godot's humanoid profile is needed before our people can use its clips. Clips in it: idle,
  walk, jog, sprint, crouch, sit down / sitting idle / sitting talking / stand up, swim forward
  and swim idle, driving, push, pick up from table, fixing kneeling, interact, dance, talking,
  and fighting ones we won't use. Godot drops the "_Loop" from names ("Swim_Fwd").
- The modular men and women have a skeleton but no animations in this download, so they
  stand in a T-pose until they get UAL clips.

## Louis's check
1. Open the project in Godot. Let it import (the first time takes a minute: lots of new models).
2. In the FileSystem panel open `scenes/kit_preview.tscn` and press **F6** (run this scene).
3. You see rows of models on a grey floor, one row per pack, with names on the floor.
   Move with **W A S D** (or the arrow keys), **Q / E** go down / up, hold **Shift** to go fast,
   mouse wheel zooms.
4. Check: the stoves, fridges and shelves face you; food sits on the grey counters; the
   horses, donkey, cow, alpaca and dog breathe (idle animation); palms are about 6 m tall,
   people about 1.85 m.
5. Press F5 and play a normal shift: nothing in the game has changed yet.
