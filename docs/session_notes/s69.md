# Session 69: HUD, meters, clock and shift summary

The gameplay HUD now looks like Louis's mockup (docs/polish/after_hud_mock.svg): three chips at
the top left, a fuller clock at the top middle, the meters at the bottom middle with icons and
words, a tidy speed panel, a "hold E" ring in the middle of the screen, and a shift summary that
counts up like a payout screen. Every part has its own GameConfig switch; with a switch off you
get the old look back (nothing old was deleted).

## What works

- **H1 chips** (`scripts/hud_chips.gd`, a child of the `HUD` layer, so the Delivered card and the
  shops still hide it with the HUD). WALLET, RATING (drawn star, "New" before your first
  delivery) and LEVEL (XP bar, "340 / 500 XP", "3 in a row · Snackaroo rider"). Money counts up
  over 0.6 s with a tick, and a green "+15" (or red "-7" for a bill) floats up next to the wallet.
  A level-up flashes the level chip gold 3 times, pops it and plays `level_up`.
  The chips sit in a flow row that wraps the LEVEL chip onto a second line when the window is
  too narrow (e.g. 1024x768 or 130% text), so they never run into the clock.
- **H2 clock** (`shift_screen.gd`): "☀ 11:42 AM", one line "Shift 3 · 41°C · Brain Freeze special"
  and a thin yellow shift bar ((hour − 8) / 10). In the last game hour the bar turns orange and the
  clock pulses gently every 10 game minutes (not with Less motion). "Last orders!" and
  "Shift over" still replace the line at the end. `_clock.text` is still just the time (the smoke
  test reads it), and the sun icon is still `_sun_icon`.
  Temperature: `GameConfig.SHIFT_TEMPERATURE` = 31°C at 8, 42 at 13, 43 at 15, 37 at 18, sliding in
  between; Hot Noon days add 4.
- **H3 meters** (`meters_panel.gd`): bottom middle, three meters side by side, each with a drawn
  icon (stopwatch; snowflake for ice cream, bubble tea, iced latte and farm juice, a thermometer
  for the rest; a box for damage), the time left, the Delivered card's word
  (On time / A bit late / Late, Fresh / Okay / Not fresh, Perfect / Shaken / Smashed) and tick marks
  at 30% and 60%. A meter that drops into a worse colour flashes twice, shakes 4 px and plays
  `meter_warn`, and keeps a thin coloured outline while it's orange or red. Colour help uses
  `UiPalette.band_colour()` (blue / orange / vermilion) and adds white stripes on a red bar.
  Placement: between the mini-map and the speed panel; if three side by side don't fit there,
  they stack (one under the other); above the tutorial tip when it shows; left of a tall phone;
  300 px higher with touch buttons (same as the speed panel).
- **H4 speed panel** (`hud.gd`): "21 km/h" on one baseline (the small unit is lifted by the
  difference in the two fonts' descent), "SHIFT sprint" small at the top right, stamina as 5
  segments drawn over the old ProgressBar (kept, with empty styles, so `_bar` still works for the
  gear_ride test and touch code). Cyan, brighter with a halo while sprinting, pulsing orange when
  tired.
- **H5 hold ring** (`scripts/hold_ring.gd`, under the HUD layer): an orange ring round a little
  sand-coloured "E" key, 96 px, 90 px below the middle of the screen (lifted if the meters panel
  is in the way). It reads `DeliveryManager.hold_progress()` (read only, the same value as the
  phone's bar) and pops when a hold completes. `scripts/polish/hud_extras.gd` hands it the
  DeliveryManager when the world opens; it also finds it by itself if needed.
- **H6 Less motion**: `follow_camera.gd` ignores bumps and speed bumps (no shake) and keeps the
  normal FOV while sprinting; `speed_lines.gd` hides; the meter pulse only flashes (UiJuice.shake
  already does nothing with Less motion); summary stamps and the clock pulse don't scale.
- **H7 shift summary**: the title fades in, then the rows appear one after another, every number
  counting up from 0 with a tick, goal bonuses slam in as tilted orange stamps (`stamp` sound),
  personal bests are gold, "New best!" is a gold ribbon that pops in with `level_up`, and
  "Tomorrow: Rush Hour" is a little orange card. The list scrolls itself to the newest row.
  E (or `_next_shift()`) during the count-up jumps to the final numbers; the next E starts the
  next shift. The row texts at the end are exactly the old ones (replay_ui reads them).

## Choices I made (and why)

1. **The meters are named Time / Freshness / Damage** (the Delivered card's tile names), not
   "Fresh" like the mockup, because the green freshness word is already "Fresh".
2. **The food name is gone from the meters panel** (it's on the phone, and the mockup has none).
   The old `Title` label is hidden, not removed.
3. **Time left stays** next to "Time" (e.g. "Time 1:29 · On time"): the old panel had it and it
   helps you plan.
4. **The clock line uses 18 px text**, not the mockup's smaller caption, so it meets the plan's
   18 px body-text rule; the clock panel grows a little wider than 330 px to fit it.
5. **Wrapping instead of shrinking** at narrow sizes (chips wrap, meters stack), so text never
   gets smaller than the chosen text size.
6. **No new ProgressBars in the summary** (replay_ui expects exactly one, the upgrade bar), so
   the "Tomorrow" card and "New best!" ribbon are styled Labels in the same places as before.

## Louis's check

1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**. Start (or continue) a shift.
2. Top left: three dark chips, **WALLET**, **RATING** and **LEVEL**. Deliver an order: the wallet
   number counts up and a green **+15** floats up next to it.
3. Top middle: the clock says something like **Shift 2 · 35°C · A sunny day** with a thin yellow
   bar that fills during the shift (orange in the last hour).
4. Pick up an order: three meters appear at the **bottom middle** with little pictures and words.
   Ride over a speed bump fast: the Damage meter flashes and blips when it turns orange.
5. Stop at the door, get off (F) and **hold E**: an orange ring fills round an "E" in the middle
   of the screen.
6. Bottom right: "**21 km/h**" on one line and the stamina bar in 5 pieces (hold Shift to see it
   glow).
7. Wait for 6 PM: the summary counts up row by row; press **E** once to skip, again to start the
   next shift.
8. Pause → Settings: with **Less motion** on (Session 71 adds the button), bumps don't shake the
   camera and sprinting shows no speed lines.
