# Session 63: The spine, the farm and the stream

Built on branch `looks-s63` (worktree /home/claude/wt/s63), with looks-build (Sessions 57, 60, 61,
62) merged in near the end, so it already sits with the new gym, pools and car parks.

## What works
- **Two paths on the spine (Appendix D), north to south:** domes → red walking track 3.5 m
  (the old "Domes Path", 3 m at z −11, now at z −6.75, Session 55's red rubber texture, thin
  white edge lines, "WALK · مشي" painted every 40 m) → grey stone kerb 0.3 m × 0.12 m → raised
  planted strip 1.4 m (grass texture, low flowers and plants) → kerb → two-way buggy road 6 m
  (the old "Spine Path", same place at z 0, lighter asphalt, white dashed middle line 1.5 m
  dash / 1.5 m gap).
- **Data names:** the roads are now `Spine Buggy Road` (`kind: "buggy_road"`) and
  `Spine Walking Track` (`kind: "walk_track"`). Session 64's buggies can route on the buggy road
  by that name or kind (I didn't touch any buggy route code or the BUGGIES list). The layout is
  in `SPINE_PATHS` in the data (kerb width/height, strip width, marking spacing, dash/gap).
- Kerbs and strip stop where a path crosses (Institute Access, C1, C2 links: a "dropped kerb",
  flush, so the bike rides straight across) and in front of every dome door, where a paved
  crossing links the buggy road, the track and the dome. Like Session 62's kerbs they're not
  solid: you ride over them.
- Paved aprons from every dome door, and from the lap pool and community pool gates (read from
  Session 60's `LAP_POOL_DETAIL` / `COMMUNITY_POOL_DETAIL`), down to the track.
- **Hills:** 12 gentle hills, 0.25 to 0.5 m high, 10 to 18 m across, each with its own `radius`
  in the data (old list kept as `HILLS_OLD`). All at least 4 m from every path, pool, bed,
  building (gym, stall), court, dome, the bee yard and its flowers, and off the stream. Session
  60's two hills at x 112 and 160 are kept but made smaller (radius 6 and 7) so they stay 4 m from
  the buggy road. Steepest slope 0.11 (gentle). The hill mesh, the grass and the layout/pool tests
  all read the per-hill radius (default 10 if a hill has none).
- **Planting round every dome:** a dark-soil flower bed hugging the dome (gap at the door),
  3 date palms behind it, 2 fruit trees at its sides (pomegranate = Quaternius `Apple_4`,
  fig = `Orange_4`), 12 shrubs and flower clumps, all kept clear of paths, pools, the gym,
  courts and gates (checked against the data, so it follows the moved pools). Palms and fruit
  trees have a solid trunk. The north palm row (was z −7, now on the track) moved to z −10.5.
- **Farm beds:** each FARM_BEDS area has 3 raised beds (wooden sides 0.3 m, corner posts, dark
  soil) with bark paths between; rows of watermelon vines with striped melons lying beside,
  pumpkin vines with pumpkins, tomatoes tied to sticks, at mixed growth stages (Quaternius
  crops, 1.3× size); a black drip pipe along every row; a wheelbarrow, a watering can and a
  scarecrow at each farm. A farmer (`FieldFarmer`) walks the bark paths of the beds next to the
  farm stall and waters (tips the can, drops fall), going round the bed ends to change path.
- **Bee yard (BEEHIVES):** 8 hives on stands (two boxes and a white lid each), a low solid
  wooden fence with a closed gate, flower beds all round it, a bilingual
  "Bees at work · نحل يعمل" sign, bees buzzing (particles). **The beekeeper** (white suit,
  white boots and gloves, hat with a see-through dark veil, a smoker) walks the aisle between
  the hives, stops at one, lifts its lid with a puff of smoke, puts it back, moves on.
- **Stream:** pebbles and bigger stones along both banks, reeds (some with cattails) and tall
  papyrus clumps, lily pads (some with a pink flower) on the water. Nothing on the two bridges.
  The water already flowed (Session 30 shader). The bridges from Session 53 are untouched and
  still at both crossings (x 73 and 303).
- **Horses:** new look (scripts/horse_look.gd): rounder body, chest and rump, a mane of tufts
  and a forelock, a full tail, darker lower legs and hooves, a white blaze, a saddle cloth,
  saddle, stirrups, girth, bridle and reins, and a soft noise "hair" texture on the coat.
  Coats: Sultan bay brown, Noor grey (was cream), the arena horse chestnut; "black" exists too.
  Same legs and trot animation as before. The stable paddock horses (LandmarkBuilder,
  Props.animal) are unchanged.
- Walkers on the spine now walk on the red track (PEOPLE_ROUTES).
- Graphics: repeated plants and crops are MultiMeshes (KitProps.place_many, about 80 groups on
  the spine); small plants aren't drawn beyond 160 m and cast no shadow; banks, lines and bed
  frames are a few merged meshes. Kerbs/strips are plain boxes, so StaticBatch merges them.

## The browser version (the kits)
I didn't change export_presets.cfg. When a model file isn't there (the Web preset leaves out
`assets/kits/*`), everything falls back to shapes made in code: palms from scenes/palm_tree.tscn,
round-ball bushes and flower clumps, simple melons, pumpkins and tomatoes. So the browser build
works and stays small, it just looks plainer on the spine. If you want the real models on the web
too, the files used are: `quaternius_nature/` PalmTree_1, PalmTree_3, Bush_1, Bush_2, Plant_1,
Plant_3, Flowers, Grass_Short and `quaternius_crops/` PalmTree_4, Apple_4, Orange_4, BushBerries_4,
Flower_2, Flower_3, Flower_4, Watermelon_2/3/4/_Crop, Pumpkin_2/3/4/_Crop, Tomato_3/4 (all small
.glb files; not measured with --export-pack).

## Choices
- I kept the buggy road where the old Spine Path was (z 0), because every cluster link ends on
  it, and moved the walking track south to z −6.75 so the gap is exactly kerb + strip + kerb (2 m).
- The plan's step 1 says "2 m strip with 0.12 m kerbs"; Appendix D splits that into 0.3 + 1.4 +
  0.3. Same thing; I followed Appendix D.
- The track runs from Institute Access (x −186) to the C3 Link (x 358), so both ends join a path.
- Hills got their own size because a 20 m hill can't be 4 m from both the buggy road and the
  stream. Changed `_hill`, `_planting`, grass_field.ground_height, layout_test and pool_test
  to read it.
- The farmer and beekeeper share scripts/beekeeper.gd (job "beekeeper" or "farmer"), so it
  doesn't clash with Session 61's scripts/farmer.gd (the farm stall counter farmer).
- Plants: the Kenney flowers have cyan leaves that looked odd, so the spine uses Quaternius
  plants and flowers.
- Kept the old code: old beds/hives in spine_builder.gd behind SPINE_LOOKS_V2, old horse in
  life_looks.gd behind HORSE_LOOK_V2, old hill list as HILLS_OLD.
