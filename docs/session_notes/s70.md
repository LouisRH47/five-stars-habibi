# Session 70: Phone app and mini-map

Polish pass items P1 to P5 (docs/polish_plan.md). Everything new is behind GameConfig flags:
`PHONE_V2` (the phone and the profile page), `MINIMAP_V2` (the mini-map) and the new
`GPS_ROUTE_V2` (the arrows on the GPS line). Set one to `false` and that part looks exactly
like before. Nothing was deleted: every old node, name, signal and text the tests read is still
there.

## What works

**P1, the phone you can read at a glance** (`scripts/phone.gd` + new `scripts/phone_v2.gd`)
- Text at the 1920 x 1080 base: titles 28 px, body 18 px, the "what now" line 20 px, nothing
  under 16 px (the test checks every label).
- The status pill (WAITING / NEW ORDER / PICK UP / DROP OFF / DONE) sits in the header next to
  "Snackaroo". The card is a little wider (380 px instead of 330).
- Wallet, stars, level, XP bar, rider name and the streak line are hidden when
  `GameConfig.HUD_TOP_CHIPS` is on (Session 69's chips show them). They're still updated, so the
  smoke test's rating and level checks still read them.
- A `mode` on the phone: `waiting`, `offer`, `to_restaurant`, `to_villa` (and `done` for the
  few seconds after a delivery).
- Waiting: "Waiting for orders" with three bouncing orange dots (still with Less motion).
- Goals fold into a small "Goals 1/2 [G]" button at the bottom. They're open while you wait
  (and after a delivery), folded on a job. G or a tap opens them. The next-upgrade line folds
  with them.
- Insurance is one small line with a shield (teal tick = ON, red cross = OFF) and an [I] key.
- "Profile [P]" is a small button next to the goals button (it was a big teal bar).

**P2, the offer card** (`scripts/phone_offer_card.gd`, `scripts/countdown_ring.gd`)
- A new order slides the phone in from the right (320 px, 0.3 s, a little overshoot), then the
  old buzz shake. The card's border turns orange.
- Big "15 Dh", then "1.2 km · about 3 min" (straight line from you to the restaurant plus the
  restaurant to the villa, at 20 km/h), the pick-up → drop-off line with two dots, the food and
  special chips, the note.
- Bottom row: [Q] skip ... "18 s left" ... the E key inside a ring that runs down over the 30 s
  and turns red for the last 5. The ring reads `DeliveryManager.time_left_on_offer()`, so paused
  time doesn't count. In the tutorial it stays full and says "no rush".
- Two orders (rating 4.5+): two cards side by side, each with its key [1] / [2] and its pay big,
  then "Shop → Villa", "Food · 490 m route", the extras ("Better offer", the heart for a
  regular, the special). The bottom row says "skip both" and the ring shows the seconds.

**P3, the job view**
- The place name big (the restaurant, then the address), "180 m away" under it (updated every
  0.25 s, to 10 m; "1.2 km" past a kilometre), the two-step timeline (orange dot = now, teal
  dot with a tick = done, grey ring = still to come), the chips, the note as a speech bubble,
  and the orange "what now" line with its key as a keycap ("[E] Hold E to pick up",
  "[F] Press F to get off"), or a little arrow when there's no key ("Park near the door").

**P4, the mini-map** (`scripts/minimap.gd` + new `scripts/minimap_look.gd`)
- A dark compass ring round the map with N (orange, bigger), E, S, W that turn with the map.
- Round logo pins for every restaurant and shop (the made-up badges in assets/logos), drawn
  upright, smoothed with mipmaps.
- The stop: an orange pin with the house number (or the restaurant's logo before pick-up). Off
  the map: an orange arrow on the ring pointing the way. Under the map, on the ring, a teal pill:
  "C1 · Villa 8 · 240 m".
- Zoom with speed: 60 m from the middle to the edge standing still, 90 m at sprint speed
  (36 km/h), smoothed over 0.5 s. With Less motion it stays at the old 85 m.
- North up (`Settings.minimap_north_up`, default off = turns with you): north stays at the top
  and your arrow in the middle turns instead.
- House numbers no longer pile on top of each other (the nearest wins a spot), and none sit under
  the ring. Roads have pale kerbs and buildings a soft shadow (both in the cached ground layer,
  so they cost nothing per frame). The GPS line on the map is thicker with a dark edge.
- The static map cache is untouched: zoom only changes the transform (minimap_cache passes).

**P5, keycaps** (`scripts/keycap.gd`): little rounded keys drawn in code. Used for E (in the
ring), Q, 1, 2, G, P, I and the "what now" line. Session 69's hold ring can use the same class.

**Profile page touches** (`scripts/profile_page.gd`): the phone's white card with a dark frame,
"Snackaroo · Rider profile" in orange, a row of teal chips (Rider, Level, Wallet, Homes,
Venues), orange section titles, and the 500 stamps as little tiles (green with white text when
visited, pale when not; the + and - stay, so it's not colour alone).

**GPS route look** (`scripts/gps_route.gd`): the ground line has soft edges and white arrows
that slide along it towards the stop (a tiny shader made in code, one shared material). Works
on Compatibility (browser, iPad).

## Choices I made (and why)
- **Pill words stay PICK UP / DROP OFF**, not the plan's TO RESTAURANT / TO CUSTOMER, and there's
  no AT THE DOOR pill: phone_refresh_test reads the pill text, and "change the look, not the
  names" won. The orange "what now" line already lights up at the door.
- **The offer card is the phone itself in "offer" mode** (not a separate panel), because the tests
  need the stops, chips and note rows visible during an offer. The whole phone slides in (the
  CanvasLayer `offset`, like the buzz), so the slide can't fight the container layout.
- **Two new helper scripts** that the plan didn't list: `scripts/phone_v2.gd` (the phone's new
  look; phone.gd was already 440 lines) and `scripts/minimap_look.gd` (the new mini-map drawing).
  Both are new files, so no merge conflicts.
- **No `scripts/polish/phone_extras.gd`**: the phone and the mini-map are already in the world
  scene and in the bare-UI tests, so nothing needed attaching. The smoke check for the hooks
  expects only the parts that exist, so it still passes.
- **G for goals** is read as a raw key in phone.gd (like P for the profile), so project.godot (a
  shared file) wasn't touched.
- **Tall lines**: the Arabic fallback font makes every line very tall, so the phone's multi-line
  labels use `line_spacing` -12.
- Distances under 1 km show in metres ("490 m route"), so the choice cards still contain
  "m route" (replay_ui_test reads it) for any length.

## Louis's check
1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**. Start a shift.
2. Look at the phone (top right): bigger text, the grey WAITING pill next to "Snackaroo",
   "Waiting for orders" with three bouncing dots, your two goals.
3. Wait for an order: the phone slides in from the right and buzzes, the border turns orange,
   you see the pay big, how far and how long, and an **E** key in an orange ring that runs down.
   Wait to see it turn red at 5 seconds, or press **E** to accept.
4. Now the phone shows the restaurant's name big and "… m away" counting down as you ride.
   Press **G**: the goals open; **G** again closes them.
5. The mini-map (bottom left): a dark ring with N, E, S, W turning as you ride, round logos
   at the restaurants in the Plaza, an orange arrow on the ring when the stop is far away, and
   "Villa 7 · 180 m" under the map. Ride fast (hold **Shift**): the map zooms out a little.
6. Press **P**: the profile page has chips at the top and your stamps as little tiles.
7. If you bought the GPS: white arrows slide along the blue line on the ground.
