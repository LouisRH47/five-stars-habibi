# Session 71: Screens, menus, sounds and accessibility

Polish pass items S1 to S5 (S6, bigger touch buttons, was "only if time": not done, see below).
Everything new is behind `GameConfig.CARD_V2`, `GameConfig.START_DIORAMA` (both from Session 66)
and the new `GameConfig.SCREENS_V2` (pause blur, clinic count-up, sliding side panel, loading
rider, button feel). With them off, the old screens come back. Nothing old was deleted.

## What works

- **Delivered card reveal (S1, `scripts/delivered_card.gd`).** The card slides in from the right
  with a whoosh (the whole card layer moves, so the layout never shifts), the three tiles flip in
  0.15 s apart, the stars pop with dings a semitone higher each (1.0, 1.06, 1.12, 1.19, 1.26),
  a perfect delivery gets a tilted orange **PERFECT!** rubber stamp that slams in with a thunk and
  a little jolt, "You earned" counts up from 0 with soft ticks, coins fly out, the tip drops in
  with a bounce, and a new XP bar fills ("Lv 3"); on a new level it fills to the top, a
  "Level up!" pill pops and the level-up fanfare plays. On the Mac (Forward+, Medium/High) the
  door shot gets depth of field: things more than 3 m behind you and the customer go soft.
  It's removed when the card closes. The tiles use `UiPalette.band_colour`, so Colour help
  changes them too. The whole reveal is ONE tween (parallel steps with start times), so a skip
  is just `custom_step(99)`. Total about 3.4 s for a perfect one with a tip.
- **Skip.** A click on the card skips to the end and leaves it up. **E jumps to the end and
  carries on in one press** (see "What I chose" 1). `GameConfig.CARD_E_SKIPS_FIRST = true` makes
  the first E only skip and a second E close (Louis can try it).
- **Accessibility settings (S2, `scripts/settings_panel.gd`).** Settings now has two columns:
  "Screen and sound" (the old rows) and "Accessibility": Text size (100% / 115% / 130%), Colour help
  (with a 3-colour preview that changes), Less motion, Mini-map (Turns with you / North up).
  Saved straight away; text size is applied at once (the window's content scale). At 130% on a
  1024x768 screen the box still fits (tested).
- **UI sounds (S3).** `python3 tools/make_sounds.py ui` makes the 9 UiSounds keys in
  `assets/audio/` (ui_hover, ui_click, ui_open, ui_close, tick, meter_warn, level_up, stamp,
  whoosh): 22 kHz mono, 2 to 38 KB each, about 110 KB in all. Our own maths (sines, noise, a
  pluck), CC0, credited in CREDITS.md. They use their own random numbers, so the old sounds
  come out byte-for-byte the same if the whole script is run again.
- **Button feel (S3, `scripts/polish/screen_extras.gd` + `ui/theme.tres`).** Buttons grow 3% with
  a soft tick on hover and squash to 95% with a click when pressed. On the start screen the
  screen wires its own buttons; in the game world `attach()` adds a `ScreenExtras` node that
  wires every button there now and any added later (pause, settings, card, clinic, shop rows).
  Theme: hover 8% lighter, pressed 8% darker and pushed down 2 px, a 3 px orange focus ring.
  Panels pop in with the "open" sound and close with the softer one.
- **3D start screen (S4, new `scripts/menu_diorama.gd`).** Behind the title: a villa corner at
  golden hour (3 villas from `VillaBuilder`, the road, kerb and pavement, 3 palms), your
  Snackaroo bike parked on its stand with the orange box, and your rider in your look standing
  by it. Picking a look on the application dresses that rider too. The camera swings 8 degrees
  each way over 20 s (not with Less motion). Mac: the far houses go a little soft. Low/browser:
  75% render size, no blur, no glow. A soft sand shade on the left keeps the title readable.
  The title drops in with a bounce, "Rider to Driver" fades in, then the phone slides up.
- **Loading screen (S4, `scripts/loader.gd`).** A little rider on a Snackaroo bike (drawn in
  code: spinning wheels, legs going round) pedals along the top of the bar as it fills, and each
  tip slides in from the right.
- **Pause blur (S5, `ui/pause_menu.tscn`, `scripts/pause_menu.gd`,
  `assets/shaders/pause_blur.gdshader`).** A new `Blur` node: the game behind goes soft with a
  35% dark tint (screen texture at mip 2.5 plus 9 nearby samples, so it's still soft on the
  browser renderer). Only visible while the pause menu is up. The Paused card has a big title,
  an orange top edge, a shadow and the keys in a soft box, and pops in.
- **Clinic (S5, `scripts/clinic_screen.gd`).** The bills card pops in and each bill counts up
  from 0 in turn, the red "You pay" total last, with ticks and a thunk. The labels already hold
  the real numbers (tests that read them see the same), and closing stops the count.
- **Side / counter panel (S5, `ui/side_panel.gd`).** Slides in from 40 px to the left over
  0.25 s with a soft whoosh each time it opens (not every frame), with the palette's panel
  colour, a soft border and shadow, an orange rounded progress bar and bigger detail text.

## What I chose, and why

1. **One E closes the card.** The plan says "E skips to the end", but the smoke test (which I may
   only add to) presses E once right after the delivery and expects the card to close. So E jumps
   the reveal to its end (every sound and number done) and closes, like before. A click on the
   card skips without closing. `CARD_E_SKIPS_FIRST := true` gives the two-press version (the
   smoke check "E closes the card" would then fail, so it stays off).
2. **The PERFECT! title becomes the stamp.** replay_ui reads `_title.text == "PERFECT!"`, so the
   title keeps that text but is see-through until the stamp (a child of the title, same spot,
   tilted -8 degrees) lands on it. No "PERFECT!" twice.
3. **Panels only fade on touch screens.** The touch test measures the buttons two frames after a
   panel opens; a pop (80% size) would make them too small then, so with touch on it's a quick
   fade instead.
4. **`UiSounds` is looked up by name** (`screen_extras.sound()`), not used as a global word,
   because class_name scripts (SettingsPanel) are compiled before the autoloads exist in test
   runs, which broke them.
5. **The start screen's diorama is switchable per screen** (`use_diorama`, starts as
   `START_DIORAMA`) so the test builds both versions.
6. `counter_panel.gd` needed no change: its look comes from `ui/side_panel.gd`.
7. Theme changes (hover, pressed, focus ring) are not behind a flag (a theme can't be). Pressed
   buttons are now dark teal pushed down 2 px instead of orange.

1. Open the project in Godot and press F5. The start screen now shows a little 3D street at
   sunset behind the title: a villa, palms and your rider by the orange Snackaroo bike. The
   title bounces in, then the phone slides up. Move the mouse over the buttons: they grow a
   little and tick. Click: they squash and click.
2. Click Settings: there's an "Accessibility" column. Try Text size 130%: everything gets bigger
   straight away. Put it back to 100%. Click Back.
3. Press Continue (or Play): on the loading screen a little rider pedals along the bar.
4. Do a delivery. When the card comes in: tiles flip in one by one, the stars ding higher and
   higher, the money counts up. Get all three meters green for the big orange PERFECT! stamp.
   Press E to carry on.
5. Press Esc: the game behind goes blurry. Esc again to carry on.
6. Walk up to a restaurant counter: the side panel slides in from the left.
7. (Optional) crash hard into a wall: the clinic bills count up.
