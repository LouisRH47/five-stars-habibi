## Choices I made (and why)
- **Old rooms kept**: `GameConfig.INTERIORS_LOOKS_PASS = false` still brings back the old shop
  insides (`ShopInterior._bikes()` / `_clothes()` and the old till box are untouched, only skipped).
- **Tuk-tuk side-on** (not nose to the glass): from the door you saw only its back, now you see the
  whole decorated side, and people outside see it through the glass.
- **Sushi: two menu boards**, the right one moved to the middle so the "Pick up" sign doesn't hide
  it from the door. The kitchen sign was Japanese letters our font doesn't have; now it's
  "Kitchen · مطبخ" (bilingual like every sign).
- **Burgerology neon** raised to 3.8 m so it isn't hidden behind the "Pick up" sign.
- **Bike price tags show the bike paint prices** (the game sells paint and upgrades, not bikes).
- **The 8 bikes that never move are one mesh** (one part per colour): each bike model is 47 small
  parts, so 9 bikes would have been about 420 things to draw in one room (Appendix I). The bike on
  the round stand stays separate so it can hide.
- **Smoke test, plan step 4**: the orders from every restaurant were already there (thai, sushi,
  tacos, burger included); I added "buy one thing at bikes / clothes" (paint and a cap, through the
  real ShopController: opens, cart, checkout, equipped, money right, the stand bike makes way).

## Louis's check
1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**. Start a shift.
2. Take a **Tok Tok Thai**, **Sushi You**, **Mario Bonito** or **Burgerology** order (south row of
   the Plaza). Get off (**F**) and walk in: Thai has the tuk-tuk on the left and woks on fire at the

   Burgerology has the pink neon sign and the grill. Walk to the "Pick up" sign and **hold E**.

   mechanic at the workbench, a bike on the round stand. Walk to the counter and **hold E**: your
   own bike appears on the stand. Press **Esc** to leave: the shop's bike is back.
4. Walk into **Dune Drip** (south row): caps wall, sunglasses, trainers, rails, mannequins in the
   window. Hold E at the counter, try a cap with A/D, then Esc.
