# Session 60 — Gym and pools (4 November 2026)

Built by Claude in a cloud session, on the `looks-s60-61` branch (from `looks-build`, Sessions 52–55).

## What works

- **A real gym, smaller, with the lap pool right in front of it.** The gym is now 24 × 13 m and
  6 m tall (was 36 × 22 × 8). Its long glass wall looks straight onto the lap pool. Inside
  (scripts/gym_room.gd), laid out like real gyms: 4 treadmills, 3 exercise bikes and 2 rowers in a
  row 1.5 m in from the glass, facing the pool; free weights in their own zone away from the glass
  on black rubber tiles (squat rack on a wooden platform with a loaded barbell, dumbbell rack,
  2 benches, 6 kettlebells); a mirror wall facing the weights with "Train hard · تمرّن بقوة";
  stretching mats in the corner; a water fountain, a towel stand, lockers with a bench; reception
  just inside the door with a curved white desk, the staff member and a smoothie cooler (the
  drop-off). People really work out: 2 runners jog on treadmills, 1 cyclist pedals (feet go
  round the crank), 1 rower slides back and forth pulling the handle, 1 lifter squats with the
  barbell, 1 person stretches (scripts/gym_motion.gd + new code poses).
- **Lap pool** (scripts/pool.gd): 25 × 7 m water, 4 lanes with blue and white lane ropes, a stone
  deck, a white edge, moving water (the stream's ripple shader in pool blue), a ladder at each end,
  a bench by the gate, and a 1.2 m glass fence all round. The gym's glass wall closes the side
  next to the gym. One 1 m gate, facing the spine paths.
- **Community pool**, laid out like a real one (scripts/pool.gd + scripts/pool_props.gd): a 1.4 m
  metal-bar fence all round with one small 1 m gate, a paved deck, the main pool 25 × 12 m, a
  kids' pool 8 × 6 m right beside the loungers with 2 shade sails over it, 10 loungers in a row
  with 4 umbrellas between them, the lifeguard on a tall chair in the middle of the other long
  side, an outdoor shower, a 4 × 3 m changing hut ("Changing rooms · غرف تبديل الملابس"), a
  bilingual pool rules sign by the gate, 2 palms in planters and 2 little tables for parents.
- **Swimmers really swim** (scripts/swimmer.gd + the new "swim" pose in person_pose.gd): front crawl
  with the arms going round one after the other, kicking legs and the head turning to breathe;
  they swim lengths in their own lane, slow down and turn at the wall, and sometimes rest at the
  wall (standing up, water to the shoulders) for 3–6 seconds. Lap pool: 3 swimmers. Community
  pool: 4 swimmers, 3 kids splashing in the kids' pool (with little splash drops), 5 people on
  the loungers (3 lying flat on their backs, 2 sitting up with a drink), the lifeguard, and 2
  people strolling round the deck. The school's two pools also get real swimmers (2 each).
- **Hills keep clear.** No hill is within 4 m of either pool or the gym (the pools are now on the
  other side of the spine path from every hill). Two new gentle hills (0.4 and 0.35 m) fill the
  lawn where the old gym and lap pool were.
- Pools far from the camera (more than about 90 m) stop moving their people, so they cost nothing.

## What I chose and why

- **Where the gym and pool went.** The plan asks for the pool between the gym's glass and the spine
  path. On the south side of the spine there's only about 18 m between the spine path and the
  stream, too narrow for a 13 m gym plus an 11 m pool (the old gym actually sat over the stream).
  So the gym and lap pool moved to the **north side of the spine, between the domes at x 70 and
  160**: the gym's rounded back with the door faces the Plaza to C1 road (you park there and walk
  in), its glass wall faces south onto the pool, and the pool's gate faces the domes path and the
  spine path. Same x as before (115), so it's in the same part of the spine.
- **Community pool moved east** (to x 270–320, between the domes at x 250 and 340). Where it was, the
  domes path and the C2 link road ran straight through it. Now no road or path crosses it.
- **Lap pool water is 25 × 7 m** (4 lanes of 1.75 m) and the decks are 1.6–1.8 m on the long sides
  (2 m at the ends), not 8 m and 2 m all round: the strip between the road and the domes path is
  24.5 m, and the gym needs 13 of it.
- **Gym 24 × 13 m** (not 14): same reason. The rounded side (Appendix E's "rounded back") is the
  street side with the door; the glass side faces the pool.
- **Reception by the door.** VILLAS 904 (and 903, see Session 61) now have their own "counter" spot;
  VenueBuilder.counter_of() uses it (only when the new rooms are on), so you walk 5 m in to the
  desk instead of to the middle of the room.
- **No UAL animations yet** (Session 56 isn't built on this branch), so swimming, rowing, squatting,
  stretching, splashing and typing are poses made in code (scripts/person_pose.gd), like the
  ragdoll. When the retargeted animations arrive they can replace them.
- **The water is opaque** (like the stream), so the parts of the swimmers under the surface are
  hidden, and the deck isn't solid (you walk on it; it's only 10 cm high).
