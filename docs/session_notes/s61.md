# Session 61 — C Institute, stable office, farm stall and school booth (4 November 2026)

Built by Claude in a cloud session, on the `looks-s60-61` branch (from `looks-build`, Sessions 52–55).

## What works

- **The C Institute lobby** (scripts/institute_lobby.gd), the approved design: two storeys tall with
  glass on both long sides (one faces the spine, so you see right through to the domes and
  palms); a balcony walkway at 5 m along the back with a glass railing, slim columns and a
  staircase up to it (someone up there watching the talk); four wide wooden steps with coloured
  cushions and 5 people on laptops (typing, looking down), all facing the **talk screen**, which
  glows and changes every 15 seconds: "Talk today · 4 PM", "Solar cities of tomorrow",
  "Welcome · أهلاً" (scripts/institute_screen.gd), with the speaker on a little stage; hanging
  plants; a wood-slat ceiling; two lounge corners with sofas; big plants; and the **curved white
  reception desk** in front of a white wall with a big teal "C" and the name in Arabic and
  English. The drop-off is at the desk, about 8 m in from the door (it was 22 m in, in the middle
  of the room).
- **The stable office** (scripts/stable_office.gd) is a little wooden room you can look into: plank
  walls, a pitched roof, an open wooden door and windows. Inside: the groom sitting at his desk
  (papers, a mug), two saddles on stands, rosettes and three framed horse photos on the wall,
  riding helmets on hooks and a hay bale. Its side window looks onto a small fenced paddock with a
  horse and a water trough. The drop-off is still in front of the door.
- **The farm stall's farmer** (scripts/farmer.gd): straw hat, green shirt and an apron, standing
  behind the counter at the stall's spot from Session 53 (4, 0, 17). He turns to look at you when
  you come close, and **he hands you the order with the side panel, like a restaurant** (he waves
  and holds the bag while you hold E). The stall also got a juice press with a jug and oranges, a
  glass cooler of juice bottles, crates of watermelons, pumpkins, tomatoes, lemons and dates in
  front of the counter, and an A-frame **chalkboard menu**: "Menu · القائمة", Farm juice 12,
  Salad box 22, Dates 15.
- **Glass security booths** (checkpoint.gd `glass_booth()`): glass all round in white frames on a
  low stone base, a flat roof with a teal band, a desk with a screen and a phone. At the school
  gate the guard sits inside at the desk, the booth has a bilingual "Security · الأمن" sign, and a
  red-and-white barrier arm stands raised by the gateway. The two community gate checkpoints use
  the same booth (their waving guard stays outside).

## What I chose and why

- **The school booth moved beside the gate** (x −357.4 instead of −365): it stood right in the
  middle of the 8 m gateway, so the barrier couldn't go there. It's still between School Road and
  the school fence, and school orders still go to it (VILLAS 901, size now 3.2 × 2.9 × 3.2).
- **The stable office moved 1.5 m north** (z 188.5): it poked 1 m into Stables Road.
- **The stable office isn't a walk-in room** (no new gameplay): you can see inside through the open
  door and the windows; the groom customer still comes out to the door like before.
- **The farmer uses the counter side panel** through a new "counter_handoff" mark on the stall
  (one line in counter_panel.gd), not the "walk_in" mark, so nothing treats the open stall as a
  room you're inside.
- **No Kenney or Quaternius models yet** (Session 56 isn't built on this branch), so the crates,
  fruit, juice press and cooler are simple shapes made in code. They can be swapped for kit
  models later.
- **The teacher waiting at the school gate** is the existing customer who comes to the booth for
  school orders (Session 32); no extra person stands there all day.
- The old outdoor stepped seats in front of the institute (Session 52) are kept.
