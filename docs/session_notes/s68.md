# Session 68: Water, beam, road wear and particles

Polish pass items W1 to W6 (docs/polish_plan.md). Everything is added by
`scripts/polish/world_extras.gd` (the Session 66 hook) or, for the beam and skid marks, by the
one spot the plan allows in `delivery_manager.gd` and by `tyre_dust.gd`. Every part has a
GameConfig switch; with it off, the old look comes back.

## What works

- **W1 the delivery beam** (`scripts/arrival_marker.gd`, `assets/shaders/beam.gdshader`,
  `assets/shaders/marker_ring.gdshader`). A soft light column that fades out towards the top,
  with faint stripes drifting up and a brighter edge, 16 sparkles rising inside it, and a
  glowing ring on the ground (1.6 m, pulsing ±10% once a second, shrinking to 1.1 m when the
  rider is within 8 m). **Orange** on the way to the restaurant, **teal** on the way to the
  customer (the phone's teal hue, made bright enough to glow). When you pick up or deliver,
  the ring flies out to 4 m and fades, a ring of sparkles bursts and the column fades away
  (a separate `MarkerBurst` node that keeps going while the Delivered card pauses the game and
  then removes itself). No collision. `MARKER_V2 = false` gives the old cylinder.
  - The marker is still a MeshInstance3D (the column is its mesh, the ring and sparkles are
    children), so `delivery_manager.gd`'s `var _marker: MeshInstance3D` didn't need changing.
    Only `_make_marker()` (2 lines) and one `set_step(stage)` call in `_on_stage_changed`
    changed. `set_step` does the burst itself when the stage moves on from a stop.
- **W2 water** (`assets/shaders/water.gdshader` rewritten; the old one is
  `assets/shaders/water_v1.gdshader`; `scripts/water_look.gd`). Foam at the edges broken up by
  moving noise, lighter at the edges and deeper blue in the middle, small waves that make sun
  sparkles (a `light()` with `pow(dot(reflect, sun), 64)`), and in the pools moving light
  lines (2 layers of cells about 0.7 m, "caustics"). A thin ring of foam where something stands
  in the water (swimmers, posts) comes from the depth texture. Foam fades out after 60 m.
  - `pool.gd`: one added line (`kind = 1`). `ambient_motion.gd`: **not changed** (WaterLook
    gives the fountain its own material copy with kind 2 when the world opens).
  - With `WATER_V2 = false`, WaterLook switches every water back to water_v1.gdshader.
- **W3 road wear** (`scripts/road_decals.gd`, `tools/make_decals.py`,
  `assets/textures/decals/*`, 96 KB). Sand drifts on both road edges (12 to 20 m apart on the
  car roads, twice as far on the inner roads), tyre marks where roads meet, manholes every
  40 m and drains between them on the ring road, cracks and patches, oil spots in 2 in 5 empty
  car park bays, and at the school gate a faded zebra and "SLOW / ببطء" in each lane (the
  Arabic is shaped by Pillow + raqm with Noto Sans Arabic, checked joined and right to left).
  Each decal is a 0.3 m thin box, `normal_fade` 0.5 (only flat ground facing up), fading from
  35 m (Low: 20 m). They keep away from speed bumps, road works, the gates, horse crossings,
  the bus station, side roads and every car.
- **W4 particles** (`scripts/vfx_ambient.gd`, `scripts/vfx_kit.gd`,
  `tools/make_vfx_textures.py`, `assets/textures/vfx/*`): fountain mist, steam from the bag at
  a hot-food pick-up, kitchen vent steam on the pizza and shawarma roofs, leaves drifting in the
  spine, gardeners' sprinkler arcs, splashes round lap swimmers, drops where the falaj starts,
  sand wisps across the ring road. A budget share every 0.5 s: most important and nearest
  first, within `VFX_MAX_PARTICLES` [150, 600, 1200]; further than 50 m from the camera = off.
  Low runs only the fountain mist and the pick-up steam.
- **W5 bursts** (`scripts/vfx_bursts.gd`): 40 confetti bits in 4 Snackaroo colours at the
  door on a 5-star delivery, 8 spinning gold coins on a tip, a ring of light and sparkles round
  the rider on a level-up. They run while the game is paused and remove themselves.
- **W6 skid marks and dust** (`scripts/tyre_dust.gd`): braking hard on hard ground lays a dark
  streak every 0.3 m (decals, at most 10 / 30 / 30), fading out over 20 s. Dust puffs are soft
  round blobs lit by the sun (`DUST_SOFT`).

## Numbers

All in `scripts/game_config.gd`, section `# --- Session 68 ---` at the end. New switches there:
`WORLD_BURSTS`, `SKID_MARKS`, `DUST_SOFT` (the plan's `MARKER_V2`, `WATER_V2`, `ROAD_DECALS`,
`VFX_AMBIENT` were already in the Session 66 section).

## What I chose and why

- **Beam blend:** the plan said `blend_add`. Added light almost vanishes against the bright
  sand and white walls at noon, so the beam uses normal see-through mixing with a bright colour
  and a brighter edge. It's clearly visible at 10 h and 17 h.
- **Water edges from the shape, not the depth texture.** The stream, the pools and the
  fountain are thin flat shapes lying on the ground (2 to 6 cm above it), so "how deep is the
  water here" from the depth texture is the same tiny number everywhere. The edge foam and the
  deeper middle use the water's own shape instead (the stream's sine line from STREAM, the
  pool box size, the fountain radius), set by WaterLook. The depth texture is still used for
  the foam round things standing in the water. Water box sides are skipped (they showed as
  thin lines where stream pieces overlap).
- **No pool tiles exist** under the water (it's an opaque-looking box), so the caustics are drawn
  in the water's colour, which reads as light on the floor through the water.
- **Fountain:** foam at the rim and round the middle column where the water falls.
- **Steam at pick-up** follows the rider (the bag) for 3 s, rather than staying at the door.
- **Skid marks:** a bike has one track, so one line (the plan said "two lines", for a car).
- **Low decals:** Compatibility draws at most 8 decals on one surface and 64 in view, so Low keeps
  every other sand drift / crack / oil spot (and all manholes, tyre marks and school paint), never
  more than 8 per 48 m ground chunk (the chunks StaticBatch merges the ground into).

- **Scripts with a class_name don't name the GameState autoload** (ArrivalMarker copies the two
  stage numbers; VfxAmbient and VfxBursts look it up with `get_node_or_null("/root/GameState")`),
  so test scripts that compile them before the autoloads exist still work.
- **Sand drifts** are 5 to 9 m long and 1.5 to 2.2 m deep: smaller ones were hard to see.

## Known issues

- Decals and GPU particles in the browser (Compatibility) were not seen in a real browser here;
  the counts are inside the plan's limits. Session 72 should look on the iPad.
- If `POLISH_HOOKS_ON` is off, the water uses the new shader without its edge sizes (no edge
  foam, still the deeper colour and sparkles), and there are no decals or particles.

## Louis's check

1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**. Start a shift and accept an order (**E**).
2. Ride towards the restaurant: the **orange** beam is a soft glowing column with little
   sparkles going up and a ring on the ground. The ring gets smaller as you get close.
3. Hold **E** to pick up: the ring bursts outward and fades, and a puff of steam rises from your
   bag if it's hot food (pizza, shawarma...). The beam is now **teal** at the customer's door.
4. Deliver with 5 stars: confetti pops at the door (coins hop out if you get a tip).
5. Ride through the spine to the stream and the pools: white foam at the edges, deeper blue in
   the middle, sparkles where the sun hits, and moving light lines in the pools.
6. On the ring road: sand on the edges, manholes, drains and tyre marks at junctions. In a car
   park: a few oil spots in empty bays. By the school gate: a zebra and "SLOW / ببطء".
7. Brake hard (let go of pedalling and press brake at speed) on the road: a dark skid line that
   fades away after 20 seconds.
