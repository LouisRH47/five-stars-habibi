# Session 57: The Plaza outside

## What works
- **Lower shops in two joined rows.** Every shop is a 12 x 10 m room, 4.5 m high (was 18 x 16 x 8 m),
  with a 4 m storeroom behind it you can't enter (14 m deep in all). Grand Groceries is 24 m wide.
  On top there are **3 floors of flats** (3.2 m each, to 14.1 m) and a 0.6 m parapet, built as
  **one joined mesh per row** that runs over the passages too: sand walls, windows 1.4 x 1.6 m every
  3.4 m with white frames and sills, small balconies on every second window of the middle floor,
  lighter bands between floors, AC boxes and solar panels on the roof. The back of each row has
  grey service doors and storeroom windows.
- **Shop order (Appendix A).** North row (fronts face the square), west to east: 1000 Pizza,
  Brain Freeze | passage | Spill the Brew, Shawarma Supreme | passage | Bread & Zaatar, Pedal
  Point Bikes. South row: Grand Groceries (24 m) | passage | Tok Tok Thai, Sushi You | passage |
  Mario Bonito, Burgerology | passage | Dune Drip, Wahat Pharmacy (scenery, door never opens).
- **Passages** (4 m) only after every second shop: paved, covered by the flats, with a ceiling
  lamp (on after sunset), a bin and a bike rack. You can ride right through.
- **Pedal Point Bikes and Dune Drip** moved into the rows (their old separate buildings are gone
  from the data; the shop controllers, counters and previews work as before). Their insides were
  squeezed to fit the 12 x 10 m room (Session 59 will redo them properly).
- **Fronts (scripts/shop_front.gd):** glass from the ground to 3.2 m so you see inside, a sliding
  glass door that opens when you (or your bike) come within 3.2 m, a 1.1 m sign band in the shop's
  colour with its **logo** and the **Arabic and English names** (the band glows after sunset, the
  letters always shine), a striped awning in the shop's colour, and 2 to 4 outside tables with
  umbrellas in the shop's colour, with a person eating at about half of them. One bench per shop
  looks out at the Plaza.
- **Grand Groceries looks like a supermarket:** double width, two sliding doors, a big green sign,
  a trolley bay with 8 trolleys, a basket stand by the door, offer posters in the windows
  ("Fresh fruit · فواكه طازجة", "2 for 10 Dh").
- **Logos:** `tools/make_logos.py` draws our own logos as SVGs in `assets/logos/` (a badge
  `<id>_badge.svg` for the sign bands, and `<id>.svg` with the name in Baloo 2 turned into shapes
  for menus/cups later). Pizza slice in a flame, dripping cone, coffee cup with a splash, spit with
  a crown, flatbread with a zaatar sprig, basket, tuk-tuk, sushi roll, smiling taco, burger in a lab
  flask, bike wheel, sun in sunglasses, and a green cross for the pharmacy. No real brands.
- **Spill the Tea is now Spill the Brew** (سبيل ذا برو). The id stays "boba", so old saves load.
  Its foods (bubble tea → coffee) are Session 58's job (Appendix F), not changed here.
- **Landscaping (scripts/plaza_landscape.gd):** the round concrete slab is gone. Walkways 3.5 to
  4 m wide (in PAVED_AREAS) join every passage, the shops, the bus station (Plaza Link), the car
  park (east) and the Western Link (behind the south row). 28 grass beds with a low stone kerb,
  shrubs and flowers, **real grass blades** (the beds are lawns for GrassField, found by their
  grass material, so `_grass_blades()` wasn't changed), 68 date palms (every 8 to 10 m), 16
  benches looking at the beds, 1.2 m planters by the terraces, round planters with palms in the
  corners of the paved square, bins and bike racks. Only the square round the sunken square is
  paved (PlazaBuilder now paves a square, 52.5 m across, instead of the 100 m circle).
- **Mini-map:** draws the beds, the paved square, the shop rows, both shops (in their colours) and
  little tags "Bikes", "Clothes" and "+" (pharmacy) on their fronts.

## Choices I made (and why)
- **Fronts at z = -186 (north) and -104 (south)**, not -188 / -102: with 14 m deep units a north
  front at -188 would put the back 2 m onto the ring road (its kerb is at -200). Both fronts are now
  41 m from the square's centre.
- The rooms are 12 x 4.5 x 10 m (RESTAURANT_SIZE), the storeroom and flats come from PlazaRows.
  The counter, interior props and menus inside are still the old ones (Sessions 58 and 59 redo
  them); they fit in the smaller room.
- ShopInterior.COUNTER moved from (5, 0, -4) to (3, 0, -2.6) so it fits the smaller room.
- The middle sail post on the south side moved 6 m west, off the walkway from the middle passage
  to the fountain.
- Session 56 (asset packs) isn't in this branch, so everything is built from simple shapes in
  code (tables, umbrellas, trolleys, racks, bins, planters).
- `scripts/restaurant.gd` and `scripts/supermarket.gd` are only used by the old test area, so I
  left them alone; the Wahat shops are VenueBuilder + ShopFront. `sign_text.gd` needed no change
  (the bands use `SignText.make_face` with an outline, so Arabic stays joined and right-to-left).
- Old layout kept: `GameConfig.PLAZA_LOOKS_PASS = false` brings back the tall shops, upper blocks
  and round paving in the game code; the generator keeps the old list (`PLAZA_LOOKS_PASS = False`
  in tools/build_wahat_data.py, `old_restaurants()`).

- Shop fronts are built from boxes (no KayKit/Kenney props yet; Session 56 brings the packs).

## Louis's check
1. Open the project in Godot and press **F5**. Start a shift (you begin at the bus station).
2. Ride east along the walkway into the Plaza. You should see two long, low rows of shops with
   **three floors of flats on top**, joined all the way along, and a gap (passage) after every
   second shop.
3. Look at the fronts: each shop has its **own logo** and its name in Arabic and English on a
   coloured band, a striped awning, big glass you can see through, and tables with umbrellas.
4. Ride up to a door: the **glass doors slide open**. Ride through a passage to the back.
5. Find **Grand Groceries** on the south side: green sign, trolleys, posters in the windows.
6. **Pedal Point Bikes** is at the east end of the north row, **Dune Drip** near the east end of
   the south row. Get off (F), walk to the counter and hold E: the shops still work.
7. Around the square: grass beds with real grass, palms, benches, planters, no big concrete slab.
8. Play until about 17:00: the sign bands light up and the passage lamps come on.
