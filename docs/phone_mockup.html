The phone app · mock-up

Session 89. Press P and a big phone opens in the middle of the screen. The game pauses. Esc or P closes it. The small phone in the corner stays as it is for new orders. On a touch screen there is a phone button on the HUD. The colours and fonts are the game's own: teal, orange, sand, Baloo 2 and Nunito. Below: how it looks in the game, then every screen.
